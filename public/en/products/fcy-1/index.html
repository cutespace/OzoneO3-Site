School and hospital applications

This model is intended for unoccupied environmental treatment in schools, hospitals and similar facilities. Configuration and operating time should be determined from room volume, sealing, temperature, humidity, ventilation and site-management requirements.

Safety note: people and animals must leave the treatment area during ozone operation. Ventilate thoroughly afterwards and follow local safety rules and the equipment manual.
