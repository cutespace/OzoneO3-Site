ABOUT CHUN YA / SINCE 1989

Applied ozone technology
for real environments

Nanjing CHUN YA Electromechanical Technology Research Institute focuses on translating ozone research into environmental disinfection applications. Its work centres on air and water ozone equipment and solutions developed for different operating environments.

1989Established

EXPERIENCE

More than three decades of focus

Since 1989, CHUN YA has accumulated practical experience and developed its product range around air and water ozone disinfection equipment, building specialised solutions for environmental hygiene and disinfection needs.

Technology, application and product quality remain the foundation of its work, with ongoing development aimed at making ozone equipment more professional, stable and appropriate for the conditions in which it is used.

APPLICATIONS

From mushroom production to public facilities

Mushroom production

Equipment for inoculation boxes, changing rooms, inoculation rooms, spawn-production rooms, rapid-cooling and pre-cooling rooms, and mushroom growing rooms of different sizes.

Public and domestic environments

Applications include kindergartens, hospitals, universities, company canteens, cold stores and washing-machine hygiene management.

Air and water ozone systems

Equipment and application guidance are considered according to the treatment medium, site conditions and intended objective.
