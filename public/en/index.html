APPLICATIONS

Ozone treatment for production and public spaces

Equipment is selected according to room volume, sealing, environmental conditions and the treatment objective. These applications are intended only for unoccupied, enclosed treatment periods.

Mushroom production

Inoculation rooms, growing rooms and changing areas, with output and operating time matched to the production process.

Schools

Classrooms, dormitories and libraries during scheduled periods after occupants have left the area.

Hospitals and healthcare facilities

Only after professional assessment and never as a replacement for routine cleaning, disinfection or infection-control procedures.

Commercial complexes and public spaces

Shopping centres, hotels, offices and transport service areas, planned according to zoning and ventilation conditions.

Safety: people and animals must leave the treatment area. Allow sufficient decomposition time and ventilate thoroughly before re-entry, in accordance with local occupational-health and public-space requirements.
