APPLICATION / MUSHROOM PRODUCTION

Ozone equipment for
mushroom facilities

Application and selection guide for ozone generators in mushroom inoculation rooms, growing rooms and changing areas, including capacity and safety guidance.

Select by application and room volume

Inoculation rooms, growing rooms and changing areas differ in volume, sealing, temperature, humidity and treatment time. Measure the effective room volume first, then select equipment and operating time for the actual process.

Operating principles

Operate ozone equipment only while people and animals are outside the enclosed treatment area. Allow decomposition time and ventilate thoroughly. Confirm compliance with local occupational health and safety requirements before re-entry.

The capacities shown are for preliminary selection and do not replace a site assessment, the equipment manual or local regulations.
