Application / Healthcare

Supplemental treatment
for unoccupied spaces

Healthcare use requires joint review by infection prevention, facilities and safety teams. FCY-1 and FCY-3B may only be discussed for controlled spaces without patients or staff and never as a substitute for cleaning, surface disinfection or infection prevention.

This page is not medical advice and makes no claim that FCY equipment sterilizes healthcare rooms or replaces infection-control procedures. Local regulatory, occupational-safety and clinical review is required.

Review sequence

Define the room function and risk level; obtain infection-control review; remove people, animals, medicines and incompatible materials as required; prevent entry; establish monitoring, decomposition, ventilation and re-entry criteria; document every cycle.
