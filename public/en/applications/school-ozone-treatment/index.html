Application / Schools

Ozone planning for
unoccupied schools

School IAQ should start with source control, cleaning, maintenance and ventilation. FCY-1 and FCY-3B are considered only as supplemental equipment for controlled, unoccupied periods.

Discuss a school project

What comes first

US EPA school IAQ guidance presents source management, local exhaust, ventilation, air cleaning and maintenance as an integrated approach. Ozone equipment does not replace ventilation, cleaning, moisture control or pollutant removal.

Planning sequence

Measure effective room volume and HVAC zones; schedule a period with no people or animals; isolate the area and prevent entry; follow the equipment instructions; allow decomposition time; ventilate thoroughly; let the school confirm re-entry under local rules.

Scope boundaryThis page does not claim that ozone equipment replaces routine school cleaning, ventilation or public-health measures. Local regulations and school safety procedures apply.
